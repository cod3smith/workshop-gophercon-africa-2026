## Overall impression

The fixes worked, the score moved four points, but the shape shifted rather than resolved. The round screen is now good. The answer and ending screens bury their best moments below the fold. The biggest opportunity is no longer accessibility; it is order.

## What's working

1. The typed and keyboard route is real engineering: #lat/#lng are the true fields, crosshair readout throttled to 400ms, Enter/Space guarded to event.target === box, scroll-margin-bottom keeps focus clear of the sticky bar.
2. The phone round layout: photo, map and submit in one 812px screen; landscape side by side.
3. Honest data: correct weather table, Latin on hover, credit that cannot name the species, evidence shown whole, link to full size.

## Priority issues

### [P1] The answer screen buries the reward and the next step
Species name at y=949, "Next round" at y=1287 on an 812px phone - 1.3 to 1.6 screens below the verdict, seen five times a game.
Fix: lead with the reveal, then the map; sticky Next round matching .guess__bar. Command: /impeccable layout

### [P1] The ending buries its share and mis-tones a weak run
At 955/25,000 it says "Brag about it" over five black squares, and the emoji render glossy black on dark rows so the medal column is invisible when the player did worst. Share at y=1302, Play again at y=1628 in a 1966px page.
Fix: reorder to score, rank, best, share+play-again, then rows and map; retitle; CSS squares tinted by tone; stop painting the rank clay on low totals. Command: /impeccable delight

### [P2] The P0 fix cost the minimalism score
~7 controls around a working map; two readouts boxed identically to real inputs.
Fix: collapse coordinates into a details that ships open and is closed by safari.js once Leaflet is live; restyle readouts as plain text. Command: /impeccable distill

### [P2] On a phone the evidence is smaller than a thumbnail, and the map traps scroll
.plate__photo renders 341x192, smaller than the 343px home tile. The 257px map captures one-finger drags.
Fix: ~45-50vh on phone, readouts to one line, gate dragging behind two-finger or tap-to-activate. Command: /impeccable adapt

### [P2] No h1, and the rules are invisible
/play and the answer page have no h1; the verdict is a p. A share link drops a first-timer on /play with no rules.
Fix: Round N of 5 and the verdict become h1s; add one visible line stating five rounds and the scoring. Command: /impeccable clarify
